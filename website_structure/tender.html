The tender lifecycle

Award is not the end
of the story.

Each stage has two sides: the commercial value only people can create, and the baseline work a governed agentic system can carry. The thread continues through diligence, financial close and mobilisation.

01

Prepare

Where people add valueCommercial and technical leaders shape the sourcing strategy, supplier field and value opportunity. They bring the right market into a process designed to produce meaningful competition.

What the agentic system carriesRequirements, package structure, issue controls, evaluation method, roles, governance and the bidder plan.

02

Appraise

Where people add valueSpecialists investigate the exceptions, qualifications and risks that could change the deal. They spend their time on material judgement instead of re-keying returns.

What the agentic system carriesReturn mapping, completeness and compliance checks, missing evidence, exceptions and a consistent view across bidders.

03

Clarify

Where people add valueThe team holds meaningful supplier conversations, tests assumptions and resolves the issues that affect value, risk and deliverability.

What the agentic system carriesBidder questions, internal inputs, approved responses, addenda, dependencies and a controlled history of change.

04

Evaluate

Where people add valueDecision makers debate risk, value, innovation and commercial trade-offs. They challenge the recommendation and retain authority over the outcome.

What the agentic system carriesNormalised offers, consistent scoring support, assumptions, comparisons, moderation evidence and the recommendation trail.

05

Close

Where people add valueCommercial leaders negotiate the position, manage residual risk and steer the preferred offer towards contract and financial close.

What the agentic system carriesDue-diligence actions, open issues, negotiation positions, approvals, conditions, contract actions and decision history.

06

Mobilise &
hand over

Where people add valueCommercial and delivery leaders align the relationship, responsibilities and practical route into execution. The receiving team understands why the deal was made as well as what was agreed.

What the agentic system carriesRequirements, accepted qualifications, negotiated commitments, residual risks, owners and an evidence-linked mobilisation pack.

What compounds

Every package strengthens the next.

Supplier intelligence, evaluation reasoning and negotiated commitments remain available from preparation through mobilisation.
