The tender lifecycle

Award is not the end
of the story.

Each stage has two sides: the commercial value only people can create, and the baseline work a governed agentic system can carry. The thread continues through diligence, financial close and mobilisation.

01

Prepare

The team has time to shape the market, bring the right suppliers in and build a package that creates meaningful competition, rather than simply getting the documents issued.

Where agentic AI helps

  • Structure requirements and package
  • Control sourcing governance
  • Set the evaluation method and roles
  • Coordinate the bidder plan
02

Appraise

Specialists investigate the exceptions, qualifications and risks that could change the deal. Their time goes into material judgement, not re-keying supplier returns.

Where agentic AI helps

  • Map supplier returns
  • Check completeness and compliance
  • Log exceptions and qualifications
  • Track missing evidence
03

Clarify

The team can have better supplier conversations: testing assumptions, protecting competition and resolving the issues that affect value, risk and deliverability.

Where agentic AI helps

  • Maintain questions and actions
  • Coordinate internal inputs
  • Prepare responses and addenda
  • Control the change history
04

Evaluate

Decision makers spend their time debating risk, value, innovation and commercial trade-offs. They challenge the recommendation and retain authority over the outcome.

Where agentic AI helps

  • Normalise offers
  • Support consistent scoring
  • Compare assumptions, risks and value
  • Maintain the recommendation trail
05

Close

Commercial leaders focus on the negotiation, residual risk and the route to contract and financial close without losing the decisions that shaped the preferred offer.

Where agentic AI helps

  • Track diligence and open issues
  • Maintain negotiation positions
  • Coordinate approvals and conditions
  • Track contract and close actions
06

Mobilise &
handover

Delivery inherits the deal, not a folder. The receiving team understands the relationships, commitments and reasoning behind what was agreed.

Where agentic AI helps

  • Record accepted qualifications
  • Assign residual risks and owners
  • Maintain decision rationale
  • Prepare the mobilisation pack

What compounds

Every package strengthens the next.

Supplier intelligence, evaluation reasoning and negotiated commitments remain available from preparation through mobilisation.

The foundation

AI cannot fix a tender
nobody runs the same way.

If the requirements keep moving, the evidence is fragmented and decision rights are unclear, agents will reproduce that inconsistency faster. We make the commercial system explicit before asking AI to carry more of the work.

People
