Offshore wind

Win it, then develop it fast.

For offshore and floating wind developers bidding seabed leasing rounds and tenders, for example France AO10.

The problem

Winning the bid, then moving fast into development after a win, often with a partner.

What we do

Bid support: evidence, scoring logic and lessons from past rounds in one system. Then a development fast start, with an experienced offshore development lead and Ignis's AI system behind him.

Onshore wind

Same team, more projects to close.

For GB onshore wind, solar and BESS developers becoming IPPs, with small teams that will stay small.

The problem

Moving more projects through the last mile of development, route to market (CfD/PPA) and procurement (EPC and supply tenders) faster, then carrying that into build and operate.

What we do

AI bid and tender/procurement managers do the drafting, comparing and tracking, and the team makes the calls.

How we work

  1. 01

    Start with public data

    Queues, CfD results and tender rules, so we come to the first meeting with a view.

  2. 02

    A sprint

    2–3 weeks at a fixed fee, on one project's data room shared securely, in a separate workspace per client under an NDA and data agreement, ending in one decision done properly and board-ready.

  3. 03

    Bid & Tender Desk

    Then your own system, which you keep and run in-house.

Agents draft, compare and track. A named person still approves price, risk and commitments.
