Commercial workflow design

A copilot is not a shared project capability.

Individual tools help people research, draft and analyse. Adoption starts when the team can use trusted evidence, agreed checks and clear decision rights across a live project workflow.

A governed pilot

Start with one pressure point: an auction deadline, a procurement decision or an evidence request on the route to close. Map the work as it happens before choosing what AI should do.

Ignis defines the workflow, human authority, information and controls, then scopes a bounded pilot for an internal team or technology partner to build and test.

Bid and auction lifecycle

Win the round without losing the thread.

A strong response begins before the deadline and should carry its reasoning beyond it. These are possible use cases across the bid lifecycle, not a prescribed system. AI can structure, retrieve and check; people keep strategy, price, risk and final commitments.

01

Capture

Keep market signals, stakeholder conversations and early choices together while there is still room to shape the opportunity.

Where AI can help

  • Track market and account signals
  • Maintain stakeholder context
  • Coordinate capture actions
  • Build a shared opportunity picture
02

Qualify

Help leaders decide whether there is a credible route to win before the work consumes the team.

Where AI can help

  • Extract requirements and deadlines
  • Map evidence and gaps
  • Surface delivery constraints
  • Prepare the go or no-go decision
03

Shape

Give the team more room to develop the solution, differentiators and commercial position before the response fragments across workstreams.

Where AI can help

  • Map customer outcomes and win themes
  • Organise proof points
  • Coordinate partners and actions
  • Maintain the commercial position
04

Produce

Let specialists improve the argument and evidence rather than repeatedly rebuilding material they have already approved.

Where AI can help

  • Coordinate response inputs
  • Retrieve trusted evidence
  • Prepare a structured first draft
  • Trace requirements through the response
05

Challenge

Keep review time on the things that change the outcome: value, risk, price, credibility and the decisions still open.

Where AI can help

  • Check compliance and consistency
  • Flag evidence gaps and risks
  • Coordinate review actions
  • Maintain the approval trail
06

Commit and hand over

Make sure the deadline does not erase the assumptions, departures and reasoning behind what has been offered.

Where AI can help

  • Control submission and approvals
  • Maintain assumptions and departures
  • Record commitments
  • Prepare an evidence-linked handover

Tender and procurement lifecycle

Buy and commit without losing the decision trail.

A supplier decision should survive clarification, negotiation, financial close and mobilisation. These are possible use cases for a project team that needs the facts, assumptions and ownership to stay visible from package strategy to delivery.

Explore the full Tender workflow ↗
01

Prepare

Give the team space to shape the market, attract credible suppliers and agree a package that will support a better decision.

Where AI can help

  • Structure requirements and package data
  • Set evaluation roles and method
  • Coordinate the bidder plan
  • Track sourcing governance
02

Appraise

Keep specialists focused on exceptions, qualifications and risks that could alter the deal, rather than re-keying supplier returns.

Where AI can help

  • Map supplier returns
  • Check completeness and compliance
  • Log exceptions and qualifications
  • Track missing evidence
03

Clarify

Support better supplier conversations while protecting competition and preserving the history behind each material change.

Where AI can help

  • Maintain questions and actions
  • Coordinate internal inputs
  • Prepare responses and addenda
  • Control the change history
04

Evaluate

Give decision makers a clearer basis to challenge risk, value and trade-offs before they approve a recommendation.

Where AI can help

  • Normalise offers
  • Support consistent scoring
  • Compare assumptions, risks and value
  • Maintain the recommendation trail
05

Close

Keep negotiation, residual risk and conditions moving without losing the choices that created the preferred deal.

Where AI can help

  • Track diligence and open issues
  • Maintain negotiation positions
  • Coordinate approvals and conditions
  • Track contract and close actions
06

Mobilise and hand over

Hand delivery the deal, not a folder: the commitments, residual risks and rationale should remain usable after award.

Where AI can help

  • Record accepted qualifications
  • Assign residual risks and owners
  • Maintain decision rationale
  • Prepare the mobilisation pack

What compounds

Each project can leave the next one in a better position.

Evidence, assumptions, supplier intelligence, decisions and commitments become easier to find and challenge. The point is not to automate judgement. It is to stop good commercial work disappearing between people, deadlines and tools.

What has to be true

Define the project decisions before designing the AI.

A pilot does not need perfect data or a new platform. It needs a bounded workflow, trustworthy sources, named decision owners and controls appropriate to the commitments at stake.

Process

The auction, procurement or close stages and their handoffs.

Information

Approved evidence, source documents and known gaps.

Authority

Who decides, approves, signs and handles exceptions.

Governance

Checks and audit trails proportionate to the risk.

Systems

Existing tools, access and integrations the pilot needs.
