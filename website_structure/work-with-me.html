Work with me

Helping energy developers run a better commercial function.

Alongside Connecting Energy, I advise energy developers on how their commercial work gets done, so good projects reach financial close with less rework. I work with founders, CEOs, boards and commercial leaders, as an independent adviser through Ignis Leadership.

Get in touch

Where I help

The commercial work between an idea and financial close.

  • Market intelligence

    Knowing which markets, auctions and grid positions are worth your time, and keeping that view current instead of rebuilding it for every board paper.

  • Early-stage development and bids

    Deciding which sites and tenders to go after, then building bids on evidence you can reuse, from qualification to submission.

  • Route to market

    CfDs, PPAs and merchant exposure, and the trade-offs between them, worked through before they turn into a deadline.

  • Procurement

    Supply chain strategy, tender packages and contract positions that still hold when the market moves.

  • Joining it up

    A lot of time and money is lost in the hand-offs between these teams. I help set up one commercial process, so evidence and decisions carry from one stage and one project to the next.

Rethinking the workflow

Change the way the work runs, then decide where AI fits.

AI agents are changing what a small commercial team can take on. Used well, they do the searching, checking and first drafts, and give people more time for judgement, relationships and negotiation.

Adding a tool to a messy process doesn't fix it. I help teams redesign the workflow first, then decide where agents can help, with a named person accountable for every decision. The technology comes second.

How it works

It usually starts with one live problem.

A bid, a portfolio decision, a route-to-market question or a procurement round. We look at how the work runs today, agree what needs to change, and change it on the real project.

Some clients want a board adviser for the longer run. Others want a second opinion on one decision. Both are fine.

I've worked in renewables since 2013, at bp, RWE and Bouygues, on projects from early-stage development to financial close, leading bids, procurement and deals.

Need a trusted adviser on your next big move?

I advise boards and leadership teams at energy developers, from growing businesses to large companies, on commercial strategy, transformation and winning bids. Bring the decision you’re facing and we’ll talk it through.
