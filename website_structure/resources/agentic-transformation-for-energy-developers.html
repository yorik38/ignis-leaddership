Agentic transformation for energy developers

A practical guide to introducing agents

From early stage development to financial close

Inside the guide

What you'll learn

  • Start with the work, not the tool. Why transformation begins with the process, the bottleneck and the data, and why automating a broken process only gives you a faster broken process.
  • What agentic means, in plain words. Agents that plan the steps, work from your documents, check their own output and stop at the gates where a named person decides.
  • Where the time goes, from site to financial close. Why so much delay and lost value sits in the handovers between stages, and how a shared project memory helps.
  • The five step maturity ladder. How to place each stage of your lifecycle on it, and why most developers today sit on step 1 or 2.
  • Your first two weeks and 90 days. Find the two or three biggest pain points, redesign the process, then pilot and embed with your own team.

Process first. Data second. Agents third.

Who it is for. Founder CEOs, commercial, development and portfolio leaders at renewable energy developers across the UK and Europe: onshore wind, solar, battery storage and offshore wind, fixed and floating.

What it is not. A software pitch, a technical architecture paper or a case for cutting jobs. It explains, in plain words, how leaders can make agentic working pay off.
