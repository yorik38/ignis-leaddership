The podcast

Conversations with the people building the energy system.

Each episode is a straight conversation with someone working on part of Britain's or Europe's energy system: developers and deal makers, network and system operators, regulators, investors, suppliers and founders. We talk about how generation, grid and demand fit together, and what it actually takes to get things built.

The energy transition is a technical story. It's a human one too. Behind every wind farm, cable and battery there are careers, teams and communities, and they rarely get the time to explain how it really works. This show gives them that time.

Some guests will disagree with each other. I'll push back where I think the argument is weak. That's the point.

  • Generation

    Wind, solar, storage, hydro, nuclear and gas: what gets built, what it costs and who pays.

  • Grid

    Connections, the queue, interconnectors, flexibility, and the wires we already have.

  • Demand

    Data centres, industry and households, and new load arriving faster than the plug.

  • People

    The careers, teams and communities behind the work. The transition only works if it includes them.

About 30 minutes, video and audio. Prepared, curious and fair, with numbers where they help and plain English throughout.

Be a guest

Got something to say about the energy system? Come on the show.

I'm looking for people working on any part of the system, from early-stage development to operations, from the grid to the plug. You don't need a big title. Some of the best stories come from the people doing the work.

About Yorik

From boiler engineer to bid director.

I started at 16 as a plumber and boiler engineer. I've worked in renewables since 2013, at bp, RWE and Bouygues, on projects from early-stage development to financial close, and ended up leading offshore wind bids.

Connecting Energy is my independent take on the transition across generation, grid and demand. I often play devil's advocate, because the easy answer is rarely the whole story.
