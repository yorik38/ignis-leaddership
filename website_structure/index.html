What does Ignis do?

Ignis improves the commercial flow that takes a live energy project from the next gate to financial close. It keeps the bid, the tender and the close file in one governed system, so the team spends its time on the decision rather than rebuilding the evidence. Your people still own price, risk and the commitment.

Who is Ignis for?

Ignis is for the founder-CEO or commercial director of a fund-backed, growth-stage IPP. The work covers onshore solar, onshore wind, battery storage and long-duration storage, and fixed or floating offshore wind. The brief is practical: move more projects to financial close.

How is Ignis different from bid or RFP software?

Bid and RFP software stores documents and tracks a response. Ignis is the commercial system around a live project: the gates, the numbers, the approvals and the record the next project reuses. It does not replace a tool that already works. It governs the work those tools are part of.

Does Ignis work for onshore and offshore wind?

Yes. Onshore covers solar, onshore wind, BESS and long-duration storage, from grid offer to financial close. Offshore wind covers fixed and floating, from the tender through to contracts and close. Ignis runs both as one governed commercial system on your evidence.

How long does it take to see results?

The first Ignis result is a Sprint on one live gate, measured in weeks rather than a multi-year programme. You see where the commercial flow slows, which numbers are trusted, and what one governed workflow would change. Anything beyond that Sprint is a separate decision, agreed with your team.

Where should we start?

Bring one live commercial pressure: a tender, an offtake decision or evidence needed for financial close. Ignis focuses on the work and decisions around that next gate. You do not need a finished data platform before that conversation.

What does the first engagement deliver?

The Ignis Sprint reviews the gaps, maps how decisions move today and designs one governed workflow around your live work. A pilot and embedding are separate next steps, agreed with your team.

Do we need a new platform?

No. Ignis starts with your existing evidence and tools. Your team keeps ownership of its knowledge, rules and commercial decisions; any new technology is a later, deliberate choice.
