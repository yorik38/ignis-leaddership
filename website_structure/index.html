The Ignis approach

Start with one important problem. Build from evidence.

You do not need to transform the whole practice at once.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Understand the objective, workflow, knowledge, governance and adoption conditions.

  2. 02

    Prioritise

    Select an opportunity that is valuable and feasible at the organisation’s current maturity.

  3. 03

    Prove

    Test one bounded workflow with approved material, human controls and meaningful measures.

  4. 04

    Adopt

    Help the people responsible understand, trust and own the new approach.

  5. 05

    Scale

    Expand only when the evidence shows that it is useful, reliable and controlled.

People remain accountable for every important bid decision.
