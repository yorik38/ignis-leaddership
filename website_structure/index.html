Why AI agents matter

AI will change bid management. Adoption starts with the work.

General AI tools help with isolated tasks. The more significant change will come from agents performing defined work across the bid lifecycle: analysing requirements, locating evidence, preparing structured outputs, coordinating tasks and capturing learning.

Used well, agents can give bid teams more capacity for the work that wins.

Pursue more of the right opportunitiesGive experts more time for strategy and client thinkingMake every completed bid improve the next

But access to AI does not create a capable bid system. Agents need reliable knowledge, clear workflows, defined authority and human approval.

Do not begin with “Where could we use AI?”
Begin with “Which bid management problem is valuable, feasible and controlled enough to solve first?”
The adoption challenge

Technology is only one part of the change.

AI adoption changes workflows, professional roles, trust and accountability. Each part of the system has to move together.

The Ignis approach

Start with one important problem. Build from evidence.

You do not need to transform the whole practice at once.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Understand the objective, workflow, knowledge, governance and adoption conditions.

  2. 02

    Prioritise

    Select an opportunity that is valuable and feasible at the organisation’s current maturity.

  3. 03

    Prove

    Test one bounded workflow with approved material, human controls and meaningful measures.

  4. 04

    Adopt

    Help the people responsible understand, trust and own the new approach.

  5. 05

    Scale

    Expand only when the evidence shows that it is useful, reliable and controlled.
