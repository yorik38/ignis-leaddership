Questions leaders ask

Before you put AI on the work.

Do you only work with developers?

Developers and IPPs are the focus of this work. The same approach can also help EPCs, OEMs and commercial teams across energy and infrastructure wherever bids, procurement and contracts carry real commitments.

Where should we start?

With your next hard date: a round, an investment decision or a close. Map where the work waits and what that costs, then choose one bounded first move.

Do we need to choose a platform or clean all our data first?

No. Start with the commercial problem and the evidence one workflow needs. That shows which information, integrations and controls actually matter.

Can we use our existing technology?

Usually, yes. The work starts with the information and systems you already have. Add technology only where the evidence calls for it.

Does Ignis build the technology?

Ignis defines the commercial requirement, workflow, controls and pilot scope, then supports your internal team or technology partner through the build. Delivery can be scoped separately where appropriate.

What stays human?

Commercial judgement, price, customer and lender commitments, risk decisions and exceptions. AI supports defined tasks, with cited evidence and clear points where a person decides.

How do you keep our data and submissions safe?

The design starts with agreed boundaries for what AI may access and do, human sign-off before anything is released, and a record of the evidence and approvals used.

Bring your next hard date.

An auction window, a lead project heading for close, a board meeting. Tell me what is coming and we can see whether there is a governed AI move worth making before it.
