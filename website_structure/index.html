Questions leaders ask

Before you put AI on the work.

What does Ignis actually build?

Tailored, governed AI workflows around the work that moves renewable projects. A first build could help a team prepare source-linked lender evidence, track procurement questions or keep commitments visible across an auction. The aim is a repeatable capability your team can use and own.

Where should we start on a live project?

Start where pressure is real: an upcoming auction, a project moving through procurement or evidence needed for financial close. Map the work, information and decisions involved, then choose one bounded activity to improve and test.

Do we need a new platform or perfect data first?

No. Start with one useful workflow and the evidence it needs. That shows which sources need improving, what controls matter and where your existing tools can be used before adding technology.

Who builds the system, and who owns it?

Ignis can build a working pilot alongside your team, or guide your internal team or technology partner through the build. Your organisation retains its knowledge, rules and decision rights. We agree the scope, access and ownership before building; a full enterprise platform is a separate decision.

Can AI help us change what we own or operate?

Potentially. For an IPP moving from develop-and-sell to build-and-operate, AI could support new workflows or services. First test the value, economics, capability and risk of the model. AI is an enabler, not the reason to change it.

What stays human?

Commercial judgement, customer commitments, risk decisions and exceptions remain human-owned. AI supports defined tasks with clear evidence, checks and escalation points.

What will the AI Score tell us?

In about five minutes, it shows what the next project can reuse from the last, where knowledge may leak, and a first fix to test. It also uses your ranges to show the time at stake. The result appears instantly on screen. It is based on your answers, not an independent audit or peer benchmark.
