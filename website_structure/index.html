Every project starts by rebuilding the last one.

A new site gets its grid offer, and the team starts again. Last project's lessons are in someone's inbox. The evidence is spread across spreadsheets. Through procurement and route to market, senior people spend their weeks reconciling versions and comparing offers. At closing, they do it all again, under deadline pressure. The deal gets done, but the time, the margin and the memory are lost.

We think it should work differently. Imagine the next project starting where the last one left off. Today, AI agents can take on the coordination and the first drafts, so your senior team gets its weeks back for what moves the deal: margin, risk and stronger terms. Each project leaves the next one sharper, and more of your pipeline reaches financial close.

Before you put AI on the work.

Where should we start?

Bring one live commercial pressure: a tender, an offtake decision or evidence needed for financial close. We focus on the work and decisions around that next gate.

What does the first engagement deliver?

The Sprint reviews the gaps, maps how decisions move today and designs one governed workflow around your live work. A pilot and embedding are separate next steps, agreed with your team.

Do we need a new platform?

No. We start with your existing evidence and tools. Your team keeps ownership of its knowledge, rules and commercial decisions; any new technology is a later, deliberate choice.
