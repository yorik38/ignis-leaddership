Frequently asked questions

What would you like to know first?

What does Ignis Leadership do?

I help bid and tender teams find the leaking workflow, install a governed human-AI system around it, and prove the change on real or recent work before scaling.

How is this different from buying another AI tool?

Most tools automate isolated tasks. I start with your commercial workflow, then connect agents, information, controls and human approvals so outcomes improve and the team can trust the system.

Where should we start?

The readiness check can show whether a bounded pilot is sensible. Commercial Discovery then uses your evidence to name the first workflow, what not to build, and the route into a design and pilot.

Do you build systems as well as advise?

Yes. The pilot ends with the selected workflow running in your approved environment, not a design deck. It stays bounded, measured and under named human gates.

How do you keep people in control?

Decision rights, trusted sources, access controls and approval points are designed into the workflow. Your people stay accountable for relationships, judgement and commercial commitments.

Can you support both bid teams and tender evaluators?

Yes. Bid teams often start with a compliance and response matrix. Evaluation teams often start with a comparable evaluation matrix. Sources and controls change; people keep the key commercial decisions on both routes.
