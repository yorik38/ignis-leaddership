The emerging challenge

The bid function is being redesigned.

Modern bid functions must qualify rigorously, align specialists, protect margin, shape a winning story, assure commitments and learn from delivery.

Much of the preparation can now be delegated. The harder task is redesigning where people lead, challenge and decide.

The opportunity

Transform your bid practice.

In 2026, AI agents can take on more of the monitoring, research, coordination and checking that consume bid capacity. The opportunity is not simply faster production. It is to redesign the practice so people can spend more time exercising judgement, leading strategy and strengthening client alignment.
