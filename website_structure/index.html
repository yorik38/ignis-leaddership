Frequently asked questions

What would you like to know first?

What does Ignis Leadership do?

I help organisations improve how they bid for complex contracts and how they tender to award them. That includes processes, decisions, team capability and practical AI workflows.

What does discovery involve?

We examine the problem, how work happens today and what gets in the way. You leave with clear priorities and a proportionate next step.

Do you build solutions as well as advise?

Yes. I design and build focused AI agents and workflows, working with your technical team or providing hands-on development where needed.

Will AI replace our team?

AI can take on parts of the preparation and coordination. Your people remain responsible for relationships, judgement, commercial decisions and approvals.

How do you help teams adopt AI?

Through practical training using their own material. People learn how to use the workflow, check its outputs and recognise when to intervene.

Which sectors do you work in?

Energy, infrastructure and industrial markets, particularly where contracts involve significant commercial risk and coordination.

Is there a standard package?

The scope follows the problem. You receive a clear proposal and price before substantive work begins.

Do you work outside Manchester?

Yes. I work across the UK and internationally, combining remote and in-person support.
