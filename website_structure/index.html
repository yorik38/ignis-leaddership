The vision

Equip your organisation for success.

AI agents create an opportunity to redesign bid and tender work, not just speed up individual tasks. Build a shared system that moves work through the pipeline faster, carries evidence and context from one stage to the next, and makes good practice repeatable across the team. That is a bigger shift than giving each person another prompt box.

The pressure today

You know the week.

Submission is Friday. The clarification log keeps growing. Someone is still chasing a price. Your most experienced person is fixing a compliance matrix at eleven at night.

Across the table, an evaluation team has six supplier returns that don't line up, a recommendation due on Monday, and a board that will want to know why.

Either way, the calls that shape the next ten years of a contract are being made by tired people, in the last few hours.

That isn't a people problem. It's how the work is set up.

A better way to work

Build more capacity into every bid and tender.

Your people stay on relationships that win work before the tender, judgement calls, and decisions only they can make. Agents take three jobs that eat the week.

01

Production

Agents gather sources, draft the comparable view, and check what changed. The team stops rebuilding the same pack by hand every cycle.

02

Coordination

Ownership, handoffs, and commercial memory sit in one place the whole desk can trust. Nobody spends the deadline reconstructing who decided what.

03

Continuity

Evidence and context travel from bid into mobilisation and execution. Award day is a handoff, not a reset.

That is where value sits: people on what agents cannot do, agents on what should never wait for a free afternoon.

Frequently asked questions

What would you like to know first?

What does Ignis do?

I help bid and tender teams stop burning the final week on gathering, checking and comparing. We find the leaking workflow, put AI on that grind, and keep people on the judgement, relationships and decisions they have to stand behind.

How is this different from buying another AI tool?

Most tools help one person prompt faster. I start with how your team already works, then install one shared way of doing the heavy lift, so the whole desk runs the same process with a named person on every commitment.

Where should we start?

Usually with a conversation about the current operation, followed by Discovery if a structured audit would help. Discovery examines how the bid or procurement practice works today and advises how it should evolve to embed AI agents. If you want a lighter first step, take the readiness check.

Do you only advise, or do you build it?

Both. The pilot ends with the workflow running on your real or recent work in the environment your team already uses, not a slide deck. Then I help the team establish it as a shared way of working.

How do you keep people in control?

AI prepares. People decide. Go / No-Go, strategy, risk, scoring, award and submission stay with named humans. Nothing is committed without someone signing it off.

Do you work with bid teams and evaluation teams?

Yes. Bid teams often start with a compliance and response matrix. Evaluation teams often start with a like-for-like comparison of supplier returns. Same idea, both sides of the table.
