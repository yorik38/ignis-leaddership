AI roulette

Your organisation may be using AI. That does not mean it has adopted it.

AI tools are spreading faster than organisations are redesigning the work. People experiment at their desks. Central pilots demonstrate the technology. But workflow, evidence and decision rights often remain unchanged.

01

At the desk

People use general AI tools for research, analysis and drafting. Prompting, evidence and checking vary by person, while consequential commercial work remains officially too risky.

02

At programme level

Central teams launch copilots and pilots. Without a commercial workflow owner and clear operating requirements, promising demonstrations struggle to become something the business can trust and adopt.

Plenty of AI activity. Limited commercial impact.That is AI roulette: experimentation without a commercially grounded design.

Where adoption gets stuck

The business knows the work. IT knows the technology. The use case can still get lost between them.

The commercial function

What does Ignis Leadership do?

Ignis turns AI priorities into a strategy, use-case roadmap, governed workflow and build-ready pilot for complex bids and tenders. We stay involved as commercial design authority while IT or a chosen partner builds and tests it.

What is a governed agentic workflow?

A governed agentic workflow gives agents defined work, approved evidence and explicit boundaries inside a commercial process. Decisions or exceptions go to named people; strategy, price, risk, commitments and approval remain human.

How is this different from using ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot?

ChatGPT and Copilot help individuals complete tasks. A governed workflow connects those tasks across a shared process, uses agreed evidence and decision rights, and leaves an auditable record. It creates organisational capability rather than isolated prompting.

Where should a bid or tender team start with agentic AI?

Start with the business problem, not the platform. Find where capacity, evidence or control breaks, rank use cases for value, feasibility and risk, then design one bounded pilot with clear success measures.

Does Ignis build the technology?

Ignis defines the commercial requirement and remains design authority through delivery. We can work with your internal team or technology partner; a direct technical build is scoped separately where appropriate.

Do you need to choose a platform or clean all your data first?

No. Define the business problem and the evidence one useful workflow needs first. The pilot design then reveals the necessary information, integrations and controls, and whether existing tools can support them.
