The pressure today

The deadline moves closer. The uncertainty stays.

Your teams lose time reconciling assumptions, filtering tender packages and chasing inputs across the commercial lifecycle. The decisions that protect margin get squeezed into the last week.

AI makes that faster. It does not make it safer. Produce more, quicker, and the questions get harder to answer: which source was that from, who approved it, and what did we actually commit to?

That is a design problem, not a software problem. It gets solved by deciding who does what, which sources count, and where a person has to sign.

The commercial lifecycle

Give your people room to lead, challenge and decide.

AI agents give your people a head start by detecting signals, building commercial intelligence, coordinating data and preparing drafts. Your people stay focused on building relationships, shaping opportunities, challenging assumptions and approving the commitments that matter.

Governed commercial delivery framework

Know what you can trust and why.

You need a clear purpose, information you can trust and someone accountable for every result.

The governance loop
1

Define success

Agree what a useful result looks like.

So you are not the one explaining a "maybe" to the board.

2

Use trusted information

Work from your documents, evidence and policies.

So nobody finds out mid-tender that an assumption came from a chatbot, not your last project.

3

Give tools clear tasks

Set boundaries and flag exceptions.

So the gap gets flagged to you at 9am, not found by the client at submission.

↻Execute and evaluateCorrect and refine
4

Check the result

Test accuracy, completeness and usefulness.

So when someone asks where a number came from, you have an answer, not a shrug.

5

Keep people accountable

Make approvals and decision rights explicit.

So the name on the commitment is someone you can call, not a log file.

6

Learn from use

Measure what improves and what needs changing.

So a loss teaches the team something, instead of quietly disappearing into next quarter.

Frequently asked questions

What would you like to know first?

What does Ignis Leadership do?

I help you improve commercial decisions from opportunity to financial close. I redesign the workflow first, then support the right delivery route, whether that means developing a focused system with you, working alongside your internal team or overseeing a specialist partner.

How is this different from buying another AI tool?

Most tools automate isolated tasks. I start with your commercial workflow, then connect agents, information, controls and human approvals into a system designed to improve your outcomes and earn your team's trust.

Where should we start?

We start where the pressure is greatest. Commercial discovery and scoping helps you identify the friction, focus on the highest-value opportunity and define a proportionate first step.

Do you build systems as well as advise?

Yes, when a focused build is the right route. I can develop a solution with you, work alongside your internal team or help you select and oversee a specialist delivery partner. The route depends on value, capability and risk rather than a preferred technology.

How do you keep people in control?

We design decision rights, trusted sources, access controls and approval points into your workflow. Your people remain accountable for relationships, judgement and commercial commitments.

How do you make adoption stick?

Your teams help shape the workflow and use it on real work. Your leaders build the confidence and judgement to challenge outputs and improve the system over time.

Who do you work with?

I work with business development, origination, bid, commercial, procurement and award teams, particularly in energy, infrastructure and industrial markets where decisions carry material risk.

Can we start small?

Yes. We start with a bounded problem and a measurable outcome, then expand only when the workflow proves useful.
