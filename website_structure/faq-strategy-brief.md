# Ignis Leadership — FAQ brief for strategy input

## Context

Ignis Leadership is a Manchester-based consultancy. It helps organisations improve how they lead, pursue and win complex, high-value opportunities. It works with commercial leaders, heads of bids and transformation teams to improve business development and bid-management practices, and to apply AI across the bid lifecycle.

Four connected service areas: discovery, consulting and advisory, solution development, and coaching and training. No fixed solution or standard package.

Founded by Yorik Tisseau, Managing Director. 15+ years across bp, RWE and Bouygues. 50+ public and private sector bids led, from a few million to multibillion pounds. Two bid practices established. Sectors: energy, infrastructure and regulated environments. Delivery across the North West, nationwide and internationally.

The business was repivoted from executive and life coaching in 2025. The website, LinkedIn and Google Business Profile are being realigned to the new positioning.

## Why this FAQ exists

Google discontinued Business Profile Q&A in late 2025 and replaced it with AI-generated answers drawn from the profile, reviews and the website. The site's robots.txt already welcomes GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended.

So the FAQ is not decoration. It is the owned, structured surface that both search engines and AI assistants read when someone asks what Ignis does. It will sit on ignisleadership.com with FAQPage schema.

## The seven questions

1. What does Ignis Leadership do?
2. What does a discovery engagement involve?
3. Do you build AI solutions, or only advise on them?
4. Will AI replace our bid team?
5. Which sectors do you work in?
6. Is there a standard package or fixed price?
7. Do you work outside Manchester?

## What would be useful

Draft answers of roughly 40 to 80 words each, written for a head of bids or a commercial director evaluating whether to make contact. British English, no em dashes. Direct, senior in tone, no marketing filler.

Two judgement calls worth a view on:

Question 4 is the objection that decides most conversations in this market. The honest answer is that AI shifts the advantage upstream to qualification, win strategy and the judgement calls that cannot be automated, which is an argument for stronger bid leadership rather than less of it. Worth pressure-testing whether that lands or sounds defensive.

Question 6 is a friction point. There is genuinely no standard package, which is true to how the work runs but can read as evasive to a buyer who wants a number. Worth a view on how to answer honestly without losing the enquiry.
