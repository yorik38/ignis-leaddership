FORGE

Install a governed bid and tender practice—without handing over judgement.

FORGE lights the workflows that leak first, stops for human gates, and leaves a working system in your environment. It supports teams that compete for work and teams that issue and evaluate tenders.

The principle

The process layer is the product. The interface is only a window.

FORGE connects trusted sources, bounded specialist capabilities and named human gates around the way your team already works.

What FORGE is

A practice installed around people—not a chatbot bolted onto the bid.

FORGE is how Ignis designs and installs a governed way of working for commercial teams that compete for work or buy well.

You do not buy “agents” as the product. You light named outcomes: a complete compliance matrix, a comparable evaluation, a controlled Gate pack or another workflow whose value has been established.

Bid and tender entry paths

Two routes. One governed practice.

The same structure supports both sides of a tender. The source material, controls and first workflow change with the job.

Win work

Bid and proposal management

Compete for work under ITT or RFP pressure. The usual first spine is a compliance and response matrix.

  • Every requirement is captured and assigned.
  • Claims remain linked to trusted sources.
  • People retain Go / No-Go, strategy and submission sign-off.
Buy well

Tendering, evaluation and supplier sourcing

Issue packages, evaluate returns and source supply. The usual first spine is a comparable evaluation matrix.

  • Returns are compared under one controlled structure.
  • Mandatory checks and clarifications remain visible.
  • People retain scoring judgement and the award decision.

The full practice map exists behind the scenes. FORGE lights only the cells that are leaking—usually one spine first.

The operating model

From trusted sources to controlled outputs—with people on the gates.

Operators choose an outcome, not an internal agent. The FORGE Manager loads the correct protocol, coordinates the bounded work and stops whenever a named human decision is required.

Approved evidence and lessons return to the library so the next pursuit starts cleaner than the last.

What remains human

FORGE prepares the work. People decide.

AI and automation can extract, structure, compare, cross-check, trace and prepare. They do not inherit accountability.

What a pilot proves

One useful spine—not a transformation slogan.

A bounded pilot tests whether the selected outcome is faster, more complete and easier to govern in real work. Expansion is earned by evidence.

Useful

Does the team reach a complete, owned result with less reconstruction?

Traceable

Can reviewers follow material claims, scores and exceptions back to their sources?

Controlled

Do named people still hold the decisions that protect margin, fairness and compliance?

Adoptable

Can operators run the installed process in the real project folder?
