FORGE

A governed agentic system for bids and tenders.

FORGE helps commercial teams coordinate evidence, workflow and decisions without handing judgement to AI. It starts with one useful spine, installed around the way your team already works.

Check your readiness →See a worked pilot
The principle

Agents do the structured work. People make the commercial decisions.

The system can find, structure, compare, trace and prepare. Named people retain qualification, strategy, evaluation, approval and sign-off.

The operating model

One controlled flow from source to learning.

FORGE does not begin with a fleet of agents. It begins with trusted source material, one selected workflow and explicit human gates.

Each output keeps its source, owner and status visible. Useful evidence returns to the library for the next bid or tender.

Two commercial routes

Win work or buy well.

The same governed architecture serves both sides of a tender. The language, controls and first workflow change with the job.

Win work

Bid and proposal management

Compete for work under ITT or RFP pressure. A typical pilot lights the compliance and response matrix first.

  • Requirements are complete and assigned.
  • Claims remain linked to sources.
  • People retain Go / No-Go, strategy and submission sign-off.
Buy well

Tendering, evaluation and supplier sourcing

Issue packages, evaluate returns and source supply. A typical pilot lights the comparable evaluation matrix first.

  • Returns are compared under one structure.
  • Mandatory checks and clarifications remain visible.
  • People retain scoring judgement and the award decision.
How it is installed

Into the project, not around it.

The pilot is configured in a bounded project folder using the team’s approved AI environment and working documents. It is not a new enterprise platform rollout.

01 / Define

Agree one workflow and its human gates.

Map inputs, outputs, owners, trusted sources and the decisions an agent cannot make.

02 / Configure

Install the selected FORGE workflow.

Set up the project structure, operating instructions, templates and evidence controls.

03 / Pilot

Run it with operators on real work.

Measure the workflow, review exceptions and leave the team able to run the protocol.

What improves

Less reconstruction. Better-controlled decisions.

The value is not more generated content. It is a commercial process that can move faster without losing its evidence or judgement.

Clarity

One owned view of the work.

Requirements, responsibilities and status stop fragmenting across files and inboxes.

Control

Sources and sign-offs stay visible.

People can see what the output relies on and where judgement is required.

Capability

The team can operate it.

The goal is an adopted protocol that does not depend on Ignis being in the room.
