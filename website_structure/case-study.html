What a governed bid workflow looks like

  1. 01
    Qualify

    Capture the opportunity, requirements, deadlines and constraints. People make the bid or no-bid decision.

  2. 02
    Retrieve

    Agents surface approved evidence, prior answers and commitments with their sources. People choose what is relevant.

  3. 03
    Shape

    The team sets win themes and the response plan. Agents expose gaps and assemble a structured first draft.

  4. 04
    Assure

    Owners review compliance, claims, evidence and risk. Red-team decisions stay attached to the response.

  5. 05
    Submit and learn

    The approved response is assembled, commitments are handed over and reusable learning returns to commercial memory.

Human control: qualification, claims, commercial commitments, review decisions and final submission.

Tender

Clearer evaluation with the trail intact.

The buying team compares suppliers on a consistent basis and can defend how the recommendation was reached.

Challenge

Requirements, clarifications and scoring drift across documents, meetings and inboxes.

Governed workflow

The pack, questions, changes, evidence and approvals stay connected from preparation to award.

Outcome

Cleaner comparisons. Fewer gaps. A decision trail that survives scrutiny and handover.

What a governed tender workflow looks like

  1. 01
    Prepare

    Turn the buying objective into structured requirements, evaluation criteria and a controlled tender pack.

  2. 02
    Clarify

    Keep questions, answers, addenda and requirement changes linked so every supplier works from the same position.

  3. 03
    Evaluate

    Agents organise evidence and flag gaps. Evaluators score, explain their reasoning and resolve exceptions.

  4. 04
    Decide

    Compare offers consistently, expose trade-offs and build a recommendation with its supporting evidence.

  5. 05
    Award and hand over

    Record approvals, communicate the outcome and carry the decision context into delivery and contract management.

Human control: requirements, scoring judgements, conflicts, recommendation and award.
