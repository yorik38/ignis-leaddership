Who it is for

For CEOs and executives of UK renewable developers and IPPs.

  • Taking more projects into procurement and construction each year.The pipeline is ready. The question is who carries it.
  • Developing and selling, building and operating, or doing both.Different models create different handovers, interfaces and obligations.
  • Running a lean team that isn't growing as fast as the work.Capable people, and only one or two who have done it at EPC scale.
  • Being asked by the board how AI will help.And wanting an answer grounded in the work, not the tools.

The pressure

Three things are true at once, and they pull against each other.

More projects

The ambition is more projects into procurement and construction every year, not one or two at a time.

Same team

Headcount stays lean. The same few people run every tender, and most of what they know lives with them.

Bigger remit

For some teams, ownership and operations add new obligations. For others, more projects bring more packages and interfaces through the existing model.

The limit isn't the pipeline. It's how much each project takes from the same few people, and whether the next one starts from the last.
