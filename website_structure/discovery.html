Who it is for

When the pressure is clear, but the right intervention is not.

Commercial Discovery is for commercial, origination, bid and procurement leaders working on complex, high-value opportunities.

It is particularly useful when:

  • deadlines are squeezing the decisions that protect margin;
  • teams are repeatedly chasing, checking and reconciling information;
  • AI is being discussed, but there is no agreement on where it would create real value;
  • the problem feels too broad to scope responsibly;
  • you want to understand exactly what you would be buying before committing to a larger engagement.
The scope

Audit the operation, not just the software.

We examine how the bid or procurement practice works today across real work: the workflows people follow, the sources they trust, the hand-offs that slow them down and the decisions that must remain human.

That often means finding where slow decisions and unclear ownership constrain the work, or where teams lose the commercial memory needed to move from opportunity to commitment.

The review combines repeatable diagnostic, operating-model, roadmap and investment methods with evidence from your team and your work. It includes:

  • structured conversations with the people who work in the practice, typically four to six;
  • a deep dive into live or recent bid or procurement work;
  • a review of workflows, responsibilities, trusted sources, hand-offs, tools and approval points;
  • a current-state evidence base showing where time, capacity, evidence or decision quality is being lost;
  • a target view of how people and AI agents should work together across the practice;
  • clear priorities for what to change first, what can follow and what not to build;
  • an indicative investment case and staged transformation roadmap.
The output

A transformation decision your organisation can act on.

You receive a standalone decision pack. It gives leadership an evidence-based view of the current practice, the target operating model, the priority changes and the likely investment required to embed AI agents effectively.

You can use the pack to pause, take the work forward internally or move into a scoped system design and pilot.

Four workstreams

Focused, bounded and useful.

01 / Establish the baseline

See the operation as it works today.

Use evidence from your team and current work to identify where performance is being lost.

02 / Define the target practice

Show how the work should evolve.

Set out where agents can carry repeatable work, where people must decide and what foundations are required.

03 / Shape the investment strategy

Understand the likely total investment.

Estimate the phased cost from design through operation and make the assumptions and sensitivities visible.

04 / Recommend the roadmap

Know what to approve next.

Sequence the priority changes and define the most credible route from the current practice to the target model.
