Who it is for

When the pressure is clear, but the right intervention is not.

Commercial Discovery is for commercial, origination, bid and procurement leaders working on complex, high-value opportunities.

It is particularly useful when:

  • deadlines are squeezing the decisions that protect margin;
  • teams are repeatedly chasing, checking and reconciling information;
  • AI is being discussed, but there is no agreement on where it would create real value;
  • the problem feels too broad to scope responsibly;
  • you want to understand exactly what you would be buying before committing to a larger engagement.
The scope

Follow the work, not the software.

We examine one real bid or tender flow: the sources people trust, the handoffs that slow them down and the decisions that must remain human. The purpose is to find the first outcome worth improving.

That often means finding where slow decisions and unclear ownership constrain the work, or where teams lose the commercial memory needed to move from opportunity to commitment.

The review combines repeatable diagnostic, roadmap and investment-case methods with evidence from your team and your work. It includes:

  • structured conversations with the people who work in the process, typically four to six;
  • a deep dive into one live or recent bid or tender;
  • a review of responsibilities, trusted sources, handoffs and approval points;
  • a current-state evidence base showing where work slows, uncertainty builds or decisions lose their context;
  • the single named outcome most worth improving first, plus what not to build;
  • an initial view of where agents could help and where people must retain judgement and authority;
  • an indicative investment case and route into a bounded pilot.
The output

A decision your organisation can act on.

You receive a standalone decision pack. It gives leadership a decision-grade view of the first workflow, the expected value and the likely investment before committing to a pilot. Assumptions, confidence and principal cost sensitivities remain explicit.

You can use the pack to pause, take the work forward internally or move into a scoped FORGE System Design and Pilot.

Four workstreams

Focused, bounded and useful.

01 / Establish the baseline

See the work as it happens.

Use evidence from your team and current workflow to identify where performance is being lost.

02 / Select the first workflow

Focus the investment.

Choose the workflow with the strongest combination of commercial value, feasibility and readiness.

03 / Shape the investment strategy

Understand the likely total investment.

Estimate the phased cost from design through operation and make the assumptions and sensitivities visible.

04 / Recommend the route

Know what to approve next.

Compare the credible delivery routes and set out the recommended sequence of investment decisions.
