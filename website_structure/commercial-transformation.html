Commercial innovation

AI can change more than how efficiently the business works. It can change what it can offer.

The question is not simply where AI can cut effort. It is where your expertise, data and operating model could become a more valuable service for customers.

A disciplined question

Start with a real customer need and the commercial strengths the business already has. Then test whether AI changes the economics, coverage or delivery model enough to make a new offer worthwhile.

This is not a claim that every AI idea deserves a build. It is a practical way to find the few that may.

Illustrative innovation patterns

New value can take
more than one form.

These are patterns to explore, not claims about client work or promised outcomes.

/

Package scarce expertise

Turn expert judgement into a service that reaches more customers, while keeping named people at the points where accountability matters.

/

Serve uneconomic segments

Make assessment, planning or commercial support viable for customers that could not previously justify a high-touch service.

/

Create ongoing insight

Turn operational history, field information or commercial knowledge into monitoring, assurance or decision-support services.

Five viability tests

Before a new offer becomes a technical project.

A commercially sound concept has to work for customers, the business and the people who will deliver it.

/

Customer pain

Is there a real, valuable problem the customer will pay to solve?

/

Right to win

Does the business have the expertise, data, trust or access to make the offer credible?

/

Safe delivery

Can people and AI deliver the service with appropriate evidence, controls and accountability?

/

Margin

Does AI change the economics enough to make the model work?

/

Sell and support

Can the business take the offer to market and support it after the sale?

What could your business offer that it cannot profitably deliver today?
