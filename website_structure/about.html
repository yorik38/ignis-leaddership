About Yorik.

I’m Yorik Tisseau. I write, speak and advise on the commercial decisions that get energy projects built.

From boiler engineer to bid director.

I started as a boiler engineer and plumber, then worked through engineering, estimating, bids and business development. At Bouygues, RWE and bp I worked on complex energy and infrastructure projects, including offshore wind.

I started Connecting Energy to make sense of what’s happening across generation, grid and demand. Through Ignis Leadership, I advise energy businesses on commercial strategy and the decisions that take projects from opportunity to financial close.
