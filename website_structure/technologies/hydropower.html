Hydropower.

Flowing or stored water drives turbines. The operating role depends on the type of plant.

All technology guides

Where it stands

Run-of-river production largely follows river flows. Reservoir plants can hold water and schedule output within environmental and operating limits. Pumped hydro uses electricity to move water uphill, then releases it to generate later.

What to watch

Watch how operators adapt to changing rainfall and river flows, and whether upgrades can make existing assets more flexible. New schemes need to balance system value with the impact on rivers and communities.
