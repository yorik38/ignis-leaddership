Batteries.

Batteries move electricity between times of day and respond quickly when the grid needs flexibility.

All technology guides

Where it stands

Grid-scale battery deployment is growing, with lithium-ion systems widely used for rapid response and shifting energy across several hours. The amount of energy a site can store and the rate at which it can discharge are distinct.

What to watch

Look beyond announced megawatts. Connection dates, usable duration, cycling costs and the mix of services a battery can sell will shape which projects are built and how they run.
