Nuclear.

Nuclear power spans existing reactors, large new projects and smaller designs under development.

All technology guides

Where it stands

Existing reactors provide a significant source of low-carbon electricity in several European power systems. Extending the life of a plant, subject to regulatory approval, is a different decision from financing a new reactor. The European Commission is working towards initial deployment of small modular reactors in the early 2030s.

What to watch

Follow the cost and delivery risk of large builds and the evidence that smaller designs can be licensed and delivered in series. Both affect where nuclear can serve demand alongside renewables and other sources of firm power.
