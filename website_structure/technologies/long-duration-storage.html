Long-duration storage.

Long-duration storage shifts electricity over longer periods of tight supply, from several hours to days.

All technology guides

Where it stands

Pumped hydro is a mature way to store large volumes of energy over extended periods. Flow batteries, compressed air and thermal storage have different operating characteristics and are at different stages of deployment. Britain is developing a cap-and-floor framework to support eligible long-duration projects.

What to watch

Watch how much energy a project can deliver, for how long and how often the system is likely to use it. These practical questions matter for funding and for comparing technologies that meet different needs.
